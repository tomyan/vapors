# F7b: 4 samples against 1, and D2's answer (2026-11-17)

Flat-scene F7b (`docs/plans/flat-scene-f7.md`, plan items 4 to 8).
Every number below is the median of the run named, on:

```
Adapter: Intel(R) UHD Graphics 630 (CML GT2) (Vulkan, Intel open-source Mesa driver Mesa 25.0.7-2+deb13u1)
Revision: 76970230 (F7b's branch, on F7a2)
```

The runs were taken under `cargo xtask quiet hold`, at a 1-minute
load average of 1.1 to 1.8. The one-scene runs had Mesa's shader
cache disabled (`MESA_SHADER_CACHE_DISABLE=true`), so both counts
start equally cold.

## GPU time a frame

| Page | Command | 1 sample µs | 4 samples µs | 4x costs µs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3000 rows, idle (the painter's pass) | `vapors-perf 100 20 --only "3000 rows, Idle" --samples N` | 274.0 | 527.2 | 253 |
| Racer, racing (the painter's pass; the track's own renderer at 1) | `vapors-perf 100 20 --only "racer, racing" --samples N` | 4449.8 | 5191.8 | 742 |
| One-scene, 60 s (its page and its scene both at N) | `vapors-perf --scene-run one-scene --samples N` | 1541.6 | 2884.7 | 1343 |
| W10 (rounded clips) | pending F3.2: not on main when F7b ran | | | |

In the one-scene run, frame to idle was 3015.6 µs at 1 sample and
4679.8 µs at 4. No frame went over 16.7 ms at either count.

GPU time is the frame's timestamp span on the GPU, as vapors-perf reports
it. The scene's pass is its own `gpu-surface` pass until F8, so the
one-scene cost is two passes at 4 samples. F8 measures again, with the
scene in the painter's pass.

## Edges against a reference

A cube turned to show three faces, drawn by the scene renderer into a
160 x 120 backing. The reference is the same cube drawn at 8 times the
size and averaged down, which gives 64 samples a pixel. The error is
measured over the 231 pixels where the reference is part covered.

| Samples | Mean channel error | Largest |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 29.06 | 127.5 |
| 4 | 10.05 | 60.3 |

ANV and llvmpipe give the same numbers.
`scene3d-gpu/tests/gpu_scene_samples.rs` pins 4 samples at under half
the error at 1. The note named `ProjectiveMeshes` for this fixture.
The scene renderer is the 3D path the page draws today, and an edge
under projection rasterises the same way in either.

## 2D pages

Measured for F7a, and pinned in `app/tests/gpu_samples.rs`:
- Every demo with a `.pixels` file draws identical pixels at 4 samples
  and at 1.
- An SVG triangle and circle off the pixel grid differ on 51 pixels, by
  at most 29.

## Memory

At 4 samples the painter holds a colour texture of 16 bytes a pixel:
12.7 MB at 1100 x 720. The scene renderer adds its own colour texture
at its backing's size, and its depth texture grows from 4 to 16 bytes a
pixel.

## D2

D2 (a) stands: MSAA 4x for pages with a scene. The largest cost on the
UHD 630, 1.34 ms on the one-scene example, is under the 1.67 ms (a
tenth of 16.7 ms) that would have called for (b) per adapter.
