# F7b: 4 samples against 1, and D2's answer (2026-11-17)

Flat-scene F7b (`docs/plans/flat-scene-f7.md`, plan items 4 to 8),
on:

```
Adapter: Intel(R) UHD Graphics 630 (CML GT2) (Vulkan, Intel open-source Mesa driver Mesa 25.0.7-2+deb13u1)
Revision: dad11c98 (F7b's branch, on main with #480)
```

## GPU time a frame

Each command was run 3 times at each sample count, 1 and 4 interleaved,
under `cargo xtask quiet`, each run started at a 1-minute load average
under 1.5 (0.9 to 1.49). The one-scene runs had Mesa's shader cache
disabled (`MESA_SHADER_CACHE_DISABLE=true`), so both counts start equally
cold. One one-scene run at 4 samples ended at load 1.67, which
vapors-perf refuses (2938.3 µs, not used), and was run again. Each cell
is the median of a run's frames; the table gives the median of the 3
runs and their range.

| Page | Size | Command | 1 sample µs | 4 samples µs | 4x costs µs | per megapixel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3000 rows, idle (the painter's pass) | 640 x 400, 0.26 MP | `vapors-perf 100 20 --only "3000 rows, Idle" --samples N` | 294.8 (294.7 to 295.2) | 539.5 (475.7 to 539.6) | 245 (181 to 245) | 0.96 ms |
| Racer, racing (the painter's pass; the track's own renderer at 1) | 1100 x 720, 0.79 MP | `vapors-perf 100 20 --only "racer, racing" --samples N` | 4513.5 (4480.8 to 4540.2) | 5208.7 (5178.9 to 5213.5) | 695 (639 to 733) | 0.88 ms |
| One-scene, 60 s (its page and its scene both at N) | 1100 x 720, 0.79 MP | `vapors-perf --scene-run one-scene --samples N` | 1568.4 (1567.8 to 1569.0) | 2903.5 (2894.1 to 2913.7) | 1335 (1325 to 1346) | 1.69 ms |
| W10 (rounded clips) | | pending F3.2: not on main when F7b ran | | | | |

A cost's range is the 4x range's ends less the 1x range's other ends.
In the one-scene runs, frame to idle was 2855 to 3113 µs at 1 sample
and 4122 to 4971 µs at 4. No frame went over 16.7 ms at either count.

GPU time is the sum of the frame's render passes' timestamp spans, as
vapors-perf reports it. The scene's pass is its own `gpu-surface` pass
until F8, so the one-scene cost is two passes at 4 samples. F8 measures
again, with the scene in the painter's pass.

**These replace the first runs**, taken at 76970230, before #480. There,
the painter made a new 4x colour texture every frame. Those runs read
274.0, 4449.8 and 1541.6 µs at 1 sample, and 527.2, 5191.8 and 2884.7 µs
at 4. Every 4x reading here is within 2.4 % of its first run (rows 539.5
against 527.2), so on ANV the churn did not change GPU time: GPU time
counts only the render passes, and a texture's allocation is not in one.
Whether it did on the Pixel is not known (The Pixel 9 Pro, below).

## Memory

At 4 samples the painter holds a colour texture of 16 bytes a pixel:
12.7 MB at 1100 x 720. The scene renderer adds its own colour texture
at its backing's size, and its depth texture grows from 4 to 16 bytes a
pixel. On the Pixel 9 Pro at full screen, 1080 x 2400, each of the three
4x textures is 41.5 MB: 124 MB at 4x, against about 31 MB of 4x-free
targets at 1 sample (the painter's and the scene's 1x colour and the
scene's 4-byte depth, 10.4 MB each).

## The Pixel 9 Pro

Pending Tom's run by hand at 4 samples, from the served build
(`https://debian.tailf56695.ts.net/vapors/f7b-try/`, the pages
`?demo=one-scene&samples=4` and `?demo=racer&samples=4`). From this
round's build on, the demo bar's line gives the mean and the median GPU
time of each second's frames, the passes a frame sums, the step its
timestamps resolve to, and how many readings were missing and why.
Chrome documents rounding WebGPU timestamps to 100 µs (`gpu_time.rs`),
so with two passes a frame's time can be off by up to about 200 µs; the
mean averages that out over a second's frames and the median does not.

An earlier build read 917 µs at 1 sample and 3932 µs at 4, then crashed.
That build made a new 41.5 MB 4x texture every frame (#472's per-view
key, fixed by #480). The reading stands with that caveat: on ANV the
same churn left GPU time unchanged (above), and whether it cost GPU time
on the phone is not known. Neither number is a multiple of 100 µs. The
line then printed the median of the frames' summed pass times, which
under 100 µs rounding would be a multiple of 100; so either that
phone's Chrome does not round to 100 µs, or rounds otherwise. The new
line's step and its mean against its median will show which.

On aa75ddd1, natively, one-scene and the racer at 4 samples and
1080 x 2400 make one 4x texture in 600 frames. A known risk for the
phone run: `SceneRenderer` makes its 4x colour and depth textures on
each size change, so while Android's URL bar animates the viewport's
height, a new size each frame makes about 83 MB a frame (scene colour
and depth at 1080 x 2400), the kind of churn #480 fixed in the painter.
Scroll and rotate the page at `samples=4` to see whether it shows.

## D2

At 1100 x 720, D2 (a) stands: MSAA 4x for pages with a scene. The
largest cost on the UHD 630, 1.34 ms on the one-scene example (1.33 to
1.35 over 3 runs), is under the 1.67 ms (a tenth of 16.7 ms) that would
have called for (b) per adapter.

The cost grows with the pixels: 1.69 ms a megapixel on one-scene. At
that rate the 1.67 ms limit is reached at 0.99 MP (1280 x 772), and a
1920 x 1080 window (2.07 MP) would cost about 3.5 ms. D2's rule names no
size, so (a) is checked at 1100 x 720 only; whether a larger window
calls for (b) is the lead's question, not answered here.
