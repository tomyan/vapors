- PR 5's acceptance holds. The `batches` step of Scroll and Resize is
  the same at 10,000 rows as at 1000 (97,697 against 98,115; 65,835
  against 66,292). `culled` is unchanged in every row.
- Scroll's draw is 1.37 million instructions at 1000, 3000 and 10,000
  rows, against Idle's 0.93. The 0.44 million difference is building
  and writing the 5 batches that enter at the view's edges each frame
  (2 of them text: pins, resolved layouts, kept ranges). So 4c's "equal
  to Idle's within noise" does not hold: a scroll's draw is flat in the
  rows, at Idle plus 5 batches.
- Insert's draw falls to 1.02 million, flat in the rows.
- Update cost: a walk of paint order (Insert) makes the index afresh,
  and so does a move of more steps than a sixteenth of them (Resize,
  whose rows after the second all move). This adds about 2,100 to
  2,700 instructions per row to those updates: Resize +7% to +9%,
  Insert +17% to +21%. Both updates are already O(rows); splice B and C
  are the plan for Insert's. Edits that move no row (Text, and Class:
  +1%) are flat in the rows.
